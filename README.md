## A little about me

Hey, I'm **Ken** — a Mathematics and Computer Science student in Kenya who enjoys building clean, useful digital experiences.

I'm interested in the space where **good design meets solid engineering**. I like taking an idea from a rough concept to something people can actually use, while learning how things work under the hood.

- Currently strengthening my foundations in **Python, software development, and computer science**.
- Building responsive web experiences and exploring backend development.
- Exploring **cybersecurity, AI-powered tools, and workflow automation**.
- Always learning, shipping projects, and improving one commit at a time.

> My goal: write software that looks thoughtful, works reliably, and solves a real problem.

That experience reinforced something I bring to software: the best projects aren't just about making something work — they're about understanding a problem, testing ideas, and building something meaningful with other people.
